# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities privately to **support@heisto.xyz**. Do not post secrets, exploit credentials, personal data or live challenge secrets in public issues.

Include affected repository/version, a minimal reproduction on a local or non-production environment, impact and suggested mitigation. Do not test against other users, move funds, extract confidential data or execute paid Mainnet attacks without explicit authorization. No response-time SLA or bounty payment is promised.

The protocol remains upgradeable and relies on an off-chain judge signer. Published bytecode checks are not an independent audit. See [security and trust](https://github.com/heisto-dev/heisto-docs/blob/main/SECURITY_AND_TRUST.md).
